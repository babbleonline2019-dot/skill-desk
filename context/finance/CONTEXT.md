## How this team works
Finance closes the books in the first five business days of each month. Accuracy beats speed. Anything that changes a reported number gets a second reviewer.

## Systems and how to use them
### NetSuite (via the NetSuite MCP)
- Read-only for AI. Never post journal entries or change accounting periods.
- Budget vs actuals live in the "FY26 Plan" saved search.
- Use cost center codes, not department names.

### Ramp
- Vendor spend and contract renewal dates live here. The renewal owner is the Ramp budget owner.
- Read-only for AI.

### Google Sheets
- Variance narratives go in the "Close Pack" sheet, one tab per month. Never overwrite a prior month's tab.

## Glossary
- Flux: the explanation of a month-over-month variance.
- Material variance: over $25K or over 10 percent.
- Budget owner: the leader accountable for a cost center.

## Things new hires get wrong
- Explaining variances by account name instead of the business driver.
- Treating Ramp card spend as booked expense before it's coded.
- Emailing budget owners directly instead of posting in #finance-close.
