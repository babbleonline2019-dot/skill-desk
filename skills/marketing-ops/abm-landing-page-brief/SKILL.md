## When to use this
You have an account brief and need a personalized landing page outline before design picks it up.

## Steps
1. Read the account brief. Pull the company name, industry, the two pain points named, and the product line we're pitching.
2. Write a headline, a subhead, and three value blocks that speak to those two pain points.
3. Suggest one proof point from our case studies that matches the industry.
4. End with a CTA and a short note on what to personalize further if we have Salesforce notes.
